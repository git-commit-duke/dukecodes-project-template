# Prototypes

- A prototype answers one question. Its folder has a `README.md` stating the hypothesis, the result, and the date.
- Nothing under `src/` imports from `prototypes/`. Promotion means rewriting under `src/` with tests, then deleting the prototype.
- Relaxed standards apply here: no tests, no ADR, no maintainer review. That is exactly why nothing here may ship.
